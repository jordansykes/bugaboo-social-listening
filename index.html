The ITC can issue an exclusion order blocking US imports of accused products, which is a higher-stakes remedy than damages. Bugaboo Xiamen Industrial Co. is named alongside Bugaboo International B.V. and Bugaboo North America.
What changed since 28 Sept: (1) 17 Nov 2026 — the Commission declined to review Order No. 18 (21 Oct), which granted Ergobaby's unopposed 20 Oct motion to withdraw claim 3 of the '275 patent. Recommended next step: Ask counsel for the procedural schedule (Markman/claim-construction date, evidentiary hearing, ALJ initial determination), since none is published in the notices we can access. Confirm the Xiamen New Baby default on EDIS. Keep this matter separate from the Giraffe and Joolz matters in all external communications.
