This week we found that Ergobaby also filed a Section 337 complaint with the U.S. International Trade Commission on 22 Jun 2026 (supplemented 23 Jun and 8 Jul 2026); the ITC instituted Investigation No. 337-TA-1514 on 22 Jul 2026 (published in the Federal Register 27 Jul 2026, 91 FR 46944). Named respondents include Artipoppe B.V., Bugaboo International B.V., Bugaboo North America, Inc., and Bugaboo Xiamen Industrial Co. Ltd. (a manufacturing entity not previously on our radar), alongside Mabe LLC, Portier USA, Ava + Oliver, and several Chinese component suppliers. Ergobaby is asking the ITC for a limited exclusion order and cease-and-desist orders — remedies that could restrict importation of the accused products into the US, a materially more consequential outcome than the SDNY damages claim alone.
