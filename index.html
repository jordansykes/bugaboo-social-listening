In re Certain Baby Carriers — Ergobaby v. Mabe / Artipoppe / Bugaboo (ITC Inv. No. 337-TA-1514)
U.S. International Trade Commission · Respondents: Mabe, Artipoppe B.V., Bugaboo International B.V., Bugaboo North America Inc., Bugaboo Xiamen Industrial Co. + import entities

Bugaboo + Artipoppe

Artipoppe Zeitgeist carrier (+ Mabe Monarch Buckle Carrier)
Claim type

What happened: The standalone SDNY patent suit we flagged last cycle — The Ergo Baby Carrier, Inc. v. Bugaboo North America, Inc. (Case No. 1:26-cv-00315, filed 13 Jan 2026, Judge Katherine Polk Failla, alleging the Artipoppe Zeitgeist carrier infringes U.S. Patents 10,426,275 and 12,016,470) — has been superseded in significance by a formal U.S. International Trade Commission Section 337 investigation. Investigation No. 337-TA-1514 was instituted 22 Jul 2026 (Federal Register notice 27 Jul 2026) and names Mabe, Artipoppe B.V., Bugaboo International B.V., Bugaboo North America, Inc., and Bugaboo Xiamen Industrial Co. as respondents, alongside several Chinese/US import entities.
Why this changes the risk picture: Unlike a standard district court patent suit, an ITC Section 337 investigation can result in an exclusion order — a Commission order directing U.S. Customs to block importation of the accused products. That is a materially higher-stakes outcome than damages alone, since it can affect actual U.S. product availability rather than just create legal/reputational exposure. This filing also formally ties a Bugaboo manufacturing entity (Bugaboo Xiamen Industrial Co.) into the case for the first time, in addition to the two Bugaboo corporate entities already named in the SDNY suit.
New context this week: We separately confirmed the Bugaboo x Artipoppe "Kangaroo Set" capsule collaboration (launched Dec 2025, still generating press), the first publicly-confirmed commercial relationship between the two brands. This doesn't resolve what the ITC filing implies about corporate/ownership structure, but it does confirm Bugaboo Group and Artipoppe B.V. work together commercially — worth factoring into how legal frames the relationship internally.
Recommended next step: Confirm internally with legal/corporate what the actual relationship between Bugaboo Group and Artipoppe B.V. is, treating this as more urgent given the exclusion-order exposure. Monitor the ITC docket and the still-pending SDNY case (last docket activity 13 Apr 2026) for motions, preliminary determinations, or settlement. This is a distinct matter from both the Bugaboo Giraffe highchair class action and the Joolz Aer2 recall — none of the three should be conflated in external communications.
